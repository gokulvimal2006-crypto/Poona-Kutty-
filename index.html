01 / WORDS I'VE BEEN WANTING TO TELL YOU

To my favourite person,

Happy birthday, Poona Kutty. I wanted to make something that feels a little more special than an ordinary birthday wish, because you deserve to be celebrated in every possible way.

You bring happiness into the smallest corners of my life. A random conversation, a silly joke, a few minutes spent talking, or simply knowing that you are doing well can turn an ordinary day into a better one.

There are so many things I admire about you, and I probably do not say them often enough. I love your smile, your expressions, your voice, and all the little details that make you unmistakably you.

Being with you has taught me that love is also found in the everyday things: checking in, remembering a tiny detail, listening to a story about your day, and making time for each other. Those simple moments mean a lot to me because they are ours.

I do not want to fill this page with grand promises. I want to keep learning how to understand you better, celebrate your wins, stand beside you on difficult days, and make sure you always feel respected and appreciated.

When I think about the future, I picture more birthdays together, conversations that last longer than planned, laughter over silly things, and ordinary days that become precious because we share them. I would love for us to build that future with patience, trust, and plenty of love.

Today, I hope you feel proud of how far you have come. May the year ahead bring exciting opportunities, peaceful days, good health, and the little things you have been wishing for. Keep being ambitious, curious, kind, and wonderfully yourself.

Thank you for being part of my story and for giving me so many moments I want to remember. No birthday page could fit everything I feel, but I hope this one makes you smile.

Have the loveliest birthday.
All my love,
